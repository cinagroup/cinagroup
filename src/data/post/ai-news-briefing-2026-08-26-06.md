## Top 7 Stories

1. **Nvidia Has Become a Banker to the AI Boom, Putting It on Dangerous Ground.** The Wall Street Journal reports that Nvidia has increasingly taken on the role of financier for the AI buildout — providing capital, credit, and investment support to the very customers buying its chips. The arrangement has helped sustain record GPU demand, but it concentrates risk on Nvidia's balance sheet if AI infrastructure returns disappoint. With earnings approaching, questions about how much of the company's growth is effectively funded by its own money are getting harder to ignore.

2. **OpenAI's Jalapeño Chip Shows Industry-Leading Inference Speed and Efficiency.** OpenAI published first benchmark results for Jalapeño, its Broadcom-built custom inference chip, claiming industry-leading speed and efficiency in AI inference. TechCrunch notes the results position the chip as a serious alternative to Nvidia GPUs for serving frontier models at scale, while CNBC explores what the custom-silicon win means for Nvidia's data-center dominance. The milestone validates OpenAI's strategy of reducing dependence on Nvidia hardware.

3. **OpenAI's Head of Data Centers Chris Malone Departs.** The Wall Street Journal reports that Chris Malone, OpenAI's head of data centers, has left the company — the latest in a string of executive exits. The departure comes as OpenAI pushes forward with ambitious infrastructure plans, including what would be the world's biggest AI data center in Ohio. The turnover raises questions about execution risk across the company's multi-billion-dollar buildout.

4. **Apple Introduces M6 and M5 Ultra, Designed for Local AI Development.** Apple announced new Mac Mini and Mac Studio models powered by M6 and M5 Ultra chips, billing them as a big leap in performance and AI compute. Ars Technica notes the new desktops are designed specifically for local AI development, giving developers a high-performance on-device alternative to cloud inference. The move extends Apple's push to make on-device AI a mainstream workflow.

5. **Perplexity Teams Up with Nvidia on a Fully Local AI Agent "Portable Computer."** VentureBeat reports that Perplexity partnered with Nvidia to launch the Portable Computer, a device running a fully local AI agent with zero token costs. The hardware signals a growing bet that agentic AI will move off the cloud and onto personal devices. It also gives Nvidia another footprint in consumer AI hardware beyond the data center.

6. **A.I. Is Becoming So Powerful, It's Stumping Those Trying to Contain It.** The New York Times examines how frontier AI systems are increasingly outpacing the guardrails, evaluations, and governance structures meant to contain them. From red-teaming failures to autonomous attack capabilities, the piece underscores how safety research is struggling to keep pace with capability gains. It lands as regulators and labs alike search for credible containment strategies.

7. **Google Expands Gemini Enterprise for Law Firms, Intensifying the Legal AI Race.** Reuters reports Google is expanding its Gemini Enterprise AI platform for lawyers, with Google Cloud announcing iManage as an anchor partner. Business Insider frames the push as Google taking on Anthropic and OpenAI in the fast-growing legal AI market. The move signals that high-value vertical segments are becoming a key battleground for frontier AI providers.
