### 6. Google's New AI Model Hits 1,000 Tokens Per Second on Nvidia GPUs

Google has unveiled a new AI model that achieves inference speeds of 1,000 tokens per second running on Nvidia GPUs — a dramatic leap in throughput that could transform real-time AI applications. The performance milestone was achieved through a combination of architectural innovations, advanced kernel optimizations, and tight integration with Nvidia's latest hardware. At 1,000 tokens per second, the model can generate the equivalent of a full novel page in under a second, enabling near-instantaneous responses for chatbots, code assistants, and real-time translation. The breakthrough puts pressure on competitors like Anthropic and OpenAI to match both speed and quality.

### 7. Nvidia's $750 Billion AI Deal Pipeline Faces 'Circular' Scrutiny

Nvidia's staggering $750 billion pipeline of AI infrastructure deals is drawing increased scrutiny over "circular" investment patterns, where capital flows from tech giants to Nvidia for GPUs, then back to the same tech giants' cloud businesses that host the resulting AI workloads. Critics argue the dynamic inflates revenue figures across the ecosystem without necessarily creating net-new economic value. The debate has intensified as hyperscalers continue placing massive GPU orders while simultaneously investing in custom silicon programs that could eventually reduce their dependence on Nvidia. Regulators are beginning to examine whether the concentrated GPU supply chain poses systemic risks.
