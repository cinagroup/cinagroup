## Top 7 Stories

### 1. Anthropic Raises $65B, Overtakes OpenAI with $965B Valuation

Anthropic has completed a massive $65 billion funding round, pushing its valuation to approximately $965 billion and surpassing OpenAI for the first time. The landmark raise reflects surging investor confidence in Claude's enterprise adoption and Anthropic's safety-first approach to AI development.

The funding round was led by a consortium of institutional investors and strategic tech partners, with reports indicating that Amazon and Google also participated. This positions Anthropic as the most valuable private AI company in the world, edging past OpenAI's most recent valuation and signaling a shift in the competitive landscape of the AI industry.

### 2. OpenAI Launches Workspace Agents in ChatGPT

OpenAI has officially introduced workspace agents in ChatGPT, enabling multi-agent autonomous features for enterprise and individual users. The new capability allows users to deploy multiple AI agents simultaneously to handle complex, multi-step workflows across documents, spreadsheets, and project management tools.

The workspace agents feature represents OpenAI's push into agentic AI, where models don't just respond to prompts but actively execute tasks, coordinate with each other, and deliver results autonomously. Early access is available to ChatGPT Plus and Team subscribers, with broader rollout expected in the coming weeks.

### 3. Pope Leo XIV Issues Historic Encyclical Calling to 'Disarm AI'

Pope Leo XIV has published a major encyclical urging the global community to "disarm" artificial intelligence, warning of unprecedented technological threats to humanity. The document represents the most significant intervention by the Vatican on the topic of AI governance and ethics to date.

The encyclical calls for international cooperation on AI regulation, emphasizing the need for transparency, human oversight, and moral frameworks guiding AI development. It specifically warns against the concentration of AI power in the hands of a few corporations and governments, echoing concerns shared by leading AI researchers and policymakers worldwide.

### 4. NVIDIA CEO Pushes Back on AI-Driven Layoffs

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has publicly criticized companies using AI as an excuse for mass layoffs, stating that "we're scaring people" and that AI should augment rather than replace human workers. The comments came during NVIDIA's latest earnings call, where CFO Colette Kress emphasized that "AI is no longer nice-to-have" for modern businesses.

Huang's stance adds a notable voice to the growing debate over AI's impact on employment. While many companies have cited AI adoption as justification for workforce reductions, NVIDIA's leadership is advocating for a collaborative approach where AI serves as a productivity multiplier rather than a replacement tool.

### 5. Google's Gemini Spark Goes Live for AI Ultra Users

Google has launched Gemini Spark, a new AI feature now available to AI Ultra subscribers in the United States. Gemini Spark enhances the Gemini model's reasoning capabilities and introduces real-time collaborative AI features designed for both individual productivity and enterprise workflows.

The rollout positions Google to compete more directly with OpenAI's workspace agents and Anthropic's Claude enterprise offerings. Gemini Spark includes advanced multi-modal reasoning, real-time document analysis, and integration with Google Workspace tools, giving Ultra subscribers access to some of the most capable AI features available in the market today.

### 6. UN Launches AI Governance for Humanity Lab in Valencia

The United Nations has officially opened its AI Governance for Humanity Lab in Valencia, Spain, marking a significant step toward international AI regulation. The lab will serve as a global hub for developing ethical AI frameworks, coordinating cross-border governance initiatives, and ensuring that AI development aligns with human rights and democratic values.

The initiative builds on growing momentum for international AI governance, with the EU AI Act, China's AI regulations, and emerging US policy frameworks all converging on similar principles. The Valencia lab will facilitate dialogue between governments, tech companies, and civil society to shape responsible AI deployment worldwide.

### 7. NVIDIA to Unveil First Windows PCs Powered by Its Chips

NVIDIA is set to announce the first Windows PCs powered by its own custom silicon next week, marking a strategic expansion into the PC processor market. The move positions NVIDIA to compete directly with Intel, AMD, and Apple in the consumer computing space, leveraging its AI and GPU expertise to deliver next-generation AI-capable personal computers.

The announcement comes as NVIDIA's stock sits at a key support level ahead of the launch. The company's AI PC initiative aligns with broader industry trends toward on-device AI processing and edge computing, potentially opening a massive new revenue stream beyond its dominant data center GPU business.
