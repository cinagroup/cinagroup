## Top 7 Stories

### 1. G7 AI Summit: World Leaders Meet with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google CEOs

The G7 summit became a major AI policy moment as CEOs from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Microsoft met with President Trump and world leaders to discuss global AI standards and governance. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis called for a U.S.-led AI coalition to coordinate international AI safety efforts.

The meeting signaled growing recognition that AI development requires coordinated global policy, with tech leaders setting aside competition to present unified positions on safety standards and regulatory frameworks.

### 2. SpaceX Lands $30B Google AI Computing Deal

SpaceX has secured a $30 billion deal to provide Google with AI computing power, with Google paying $920 million per month for compute capacity at xAI data centers. The deal comes just ahead of SpaceX's record IPO and represents one of the largest AI infrastructure agreements to date.

The partnership highlights the intensifying competition for AI compute resources and the strategic importance of data center infrastructure in the AI race. Google's investment in SpaceX's compute capacity signals the company's commitment to scaling AI operations despite building its own infrastructure.

### 3. Nobel Laureate John Jumper Leaves Google DeepMind for Anthropic

John Jumper, the Nobel Prize-winning scientist who led Google DeepMind's AlphaFold protein structure prediction breakthrough, is leaving Google to join Anthropic. The move represents a significant talent drain from Google's AI research division, which has been considered the crown jewel of AI research.

Jumper's departure comes as Anthropic aggressively builds its research capabilities ahead of its own IPO plans. The hire strengthens Anthropic's position in fundamental AI research and safety work.

### 4. AI CEOs Warn Congress: AI Making Bioweapons Too Easy to Create

In a rare show of unity, CEOs from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft jointly warned Congress that AI is making it too easy to design and create biological weapons. The executives called for mandatory synthetic DNA screening and stricter regulation of synthetic biology applications.

The warning follows reports that AI bots have been providing scientists with information on creating biological weapons. The AI leaders are pushing for new safeguards that would require synthetic DNA manufacturers to screen orders against known pathogen sequences.

### 5. OpenAI Files for IPO After Anthropic

OpenAI has officially filed for a U.S. IPO, following Anthropic's earlier filing as both AI giants head toward public markets. The move comes as both companies seek to raise capital for massive AI infrastructure investments and research programs.

The dual IPOs represent a watershed moment for the AI industry, bringing the two leading AI safety-focused companies into public markets where they'll face increased scrutiny over governance, safety practices, and financial performance.

### 6. NVIDIA Launches NemoClaw and Enterprise AI Agent Platforms

NVIDIA announced NemoClaw, a new toolkit for building enterprise AI agents, along with a suite of platforms designed to help software leaders build secure, autonomous AI agents. The company also unveiled BlueField 4 with AI agent security software running on its silicon.

The announcements position NVIDIA as the infrastructure backbone for the emerging AI agent economy, providing the hardware and software stack needed for enterprises to deploy autonomous AI systems safely.

### 7. Meta's Internal AI Unit Described as "Soul-Crushing" by Engineers

Meta's months-old AI unit is facing internal turmoil, with engineers describing the work environment as a "soul-crushing gulag." The reports come as Meta caps internal AI token spending after costs approached billions in 2026, and as the company struggles with its AI workforce transformation.

The internal struggles highlight the challenges traditional tech companies face in restructuring for the AI era, as Meta attempts to pivot its massive workforce toward AI development while managing costs and maintaining morale.
