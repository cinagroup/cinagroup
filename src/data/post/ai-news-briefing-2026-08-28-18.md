## Top 7 Stories

### 1. OpenAI to Start Showing Ads on ChatGPT's Free and Go Tiers in India

OpenAI is introducing ads on ChatGPT's free and lower-priced Go tiers in India, according to TechCrunch. With more than 100 million weekly active ChatGPT users in India — a huge share on the free or budget tiers — the country becomes the proving ground for OpenAI's first advertising experiments on its flagship consumer product.

The move marks a significant step in OpenAI's path toward consumer monetization beyond subscriptions. India's massive, price-sensitive user base offers an ideal testbed: if ad-supported ChatGPT works there, it signals OpenAI's ambition to build a two-sided business where ads subsidize free access globally — and to close the gap with Google and Meta's ad-powered distribution models.

### 2. Amazon Tripled Its NVIDIA Chip Order Amid 'Surging Demand'

Amazon is adding another 2 million NVIDIA GPU chips to its data centers over the next two years, tripling its prior order, TechCrunch reported. The expansion reflects surging demand for AI compute, and the extended partnership goes beyond chip purchases to deeper collaboration between the two companies on infrastructure and services.

The order cements NVIDIA's already dominant position in the AI chip market and signals that hyperscaler demand shows no sign of cooling. For Amazon, it bolsters AWS's ability to offer frontier-scale training and inference capacity — critical as enterprise and startup customers race to deploy AI agents and large models.

### 3. Anthropic Continues Compute-Gobbling Streak With $45B Nscale Deal

Anthropic has signed a $45 billion compute deal with infrastructure provider Nscale, continuing what TechCrunch described as Anthropic's "white-hot compute-gobbling streak." The agreement adds to a series of massive capacity commitments as the lab races to secure the GPUs needed to train and serve its frontier models.

The deal underscores how compute — not just model quality — has become the defining constraint of the frontier AI race. Locking in multi-year capacity lets Anthropic plan aggressive training runs and enterprise deployments, while also signaling to investors that its revenue growth and capital needs remain enormous.

### 4. Barret Zoph, Thinking Machines Co-Founder, Lands at Google

Barret Zoph — who co-founded Thinking Machines Lab alongside Mira Murati, served as its CTO, and was ousted before joining OpenAI in a brief stint — is now at Google, TechCrunch reported. The move reunites him with the search giant's Gemini efforts, adding a leading post-training and reasoning researcher to Google DeepMind's ranks.

Zoph's arrival is a notable talent win for Google as competition for top AI researchers intensifies. It also highlights the turbulence around Thinking Machines, which has seen key figures depart after its high-profile founding, and adds another chapter to the industry's rapid reshuffling of senior AI talent.

### 5. Bill Gates Calls for Robot Tax and 'Human Reserved' Jobs to Mitigate AI Harms

Bill Gates is warning that AI is more dangerous than big tech will admit, writing in a new essay that "the choices we make now are critical," and proposing major limits on AI development, according to The New York Times and CNN. In a separate intervention, TechCrunch reported Gates wants to see a robot tax and "Human Reserved" jobs to mitigate the harms from AI-driven automation.

Gates' proposals — including taxing automation and reserving certain roles for humans — frame AI policy as a labor and distribution problem, not just a safety one. Coming from a figure whose philanthropic credibility spans global health, the push gives added weight to calls for governments to act before automation displaces workers at scale.

### 6. Google's AI Mode Now Tracks Flight Prices and Books Hotels

Google's AI Mode is expanding beyond information retrieval into travel transactions: it can now track flight prices, help book hotels, and more, TechCrunch reported, echoing Google's own announcement of "3 new ways to plan and book travel in Search." The updates position AI Mode as an AI travel agent that handles parts of the trip-planning and booking process end to end.

This marks a meaningful shift from search-as-information to search-as-action. By letting AI Mode execute bookings, Google is betting that conversational agents will capture a growing share of commerce — a move that intensifies competition with dedicated travel platforms and with AI assistants from OpenAI and others.

### 7. Reuters: Cybercriminals Used SpaceX's Cursor AI Tool to Hack Seven Companies

Reuters reported exclusively that Russian-speaking cybercriminals used SpaceX's Cursor AI tool to hack seven companies. The report is the latest evidence that AI coding assistants are being weaponized by threat actors to accelerate and scale intrusions — a pattern at the center of this week's industry-wide warnings about AI-powered cyberattacks.

The incident gives concrete texture to the cyber-defense pledges signed this week by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and more than 100 companies. It also raises urgent questions about the security of AI developer tools themselves: if a coding assistant can be used to break into organizations at scale, securing the AI supply chain becomes a national-security issue.
