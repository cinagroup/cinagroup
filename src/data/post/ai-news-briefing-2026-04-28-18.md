### 1. Microsoft and OpenAI End Their Exclusive Revenue-Sharing Partnership
In a landmark shift, Microsoft and OpenAI have officially terminated their exclusive revenue-sharing agreement, as reported by Bloomberg. The deal, which gave Microsoft a significant stake in OpenAI's revenues in exchange for Azure cloud credits and infrastructure support, has been winding down as Microsoft pivots toward building more of its own AI capabilities. The breakup marks the culmination of months of growing distance between the two companies, with Microsoft increasingly investing in alternative AI models and infrastructure. The story dominated Hacker News with 880 points and 760 comments, reflecting widespread attention to the changing AI industry dynamics.

### 2. Alibaba Unveils Qwen 3 — A Family of Hybrid AI Reasoning Models Rivaling GPT and Gemini
Alibaba released Qwen3 today, a comprehensive family of AI models ranging from 0.6 billion to 235 billion parameters, including 2 MoE (Mixture of Experts) models and 6 dense models. Alibaba claims the flagship Qwen3-235B-A22B achieves competitive results in coding, math, and general benchmarks, potentially matching or outperforming models from Google and OpenAI. Most models are being released under open-weight licenses on Hugging Face and GitHub, further intensifying pressure on American AI labs to deliver more capable technologies at competitive pricing. The release comes amid ongoing U.S. restrictions limiting Chinese AI companies' access to advanced training chips.

### 3. Huawei Developing Ascend 910D Chip to Compete with Nvidia's H100
Huawei is making significant progress on its latest Ascend AI GPU, the Ascend 910D, which aims to rival Nvidia's H100 series for AI model training, according to the Wall Street Journal. Huawei has been reaching out to Chinese firms to find test partners for the new chip. The development comes weeks after the U.S. imposed further export restrictions on AI chips to China. If successful, the Ascend 910D could help fill a critical void in the Chinese AI hardware market, reducing dependency on Nvidia products that are increasingly unavailable due to trade controls.

### 4. OpenAI Upgrades ChatGPT Search with AI-Powered Shopping Features
OpenAI announced a major update to ChatGPT Search that transforms it into a shopping assistant. Users can now search for products in natural language and receive personalized recommendations with images, reviews, and direct purchase links across fashion, beauty, home goods, and electronics categories. The feature is rolling out to ChatGPT Pro, Plus, Free, and even logged-out users globally. OpenAI reports that ChatGPT search usage exceeded one billion web searches last week, positioning the company as a growing competitor to Google's search advertising business.

The incident follows OpenAI's recent move to make the platform more broadly permissive, raising questions about the trade-offs between openness and safety.
