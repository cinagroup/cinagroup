## Top 7 Stories

### 1. OpenAI Closes Just $11 Billion of a Targeted $30 Billion Round at a $1.25 Trillion Valuation
Sources: [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/10/62764054/openai-raises-just-11-billion-as-ai-bubble-fears-rise-valuation-hits-1-25-trillion) · [Stocktwits via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/openai-ipo-valuation-target-reportedly-113055028.html) · [Cryptopolitan](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/technology/openai-lines-up-coatue-and-uae-money-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/ar-AA2fU99W)

OpenAI has closed roughly $11 billion in new funding — far short of the $30 billion it had been targeting — with the round valuing the company at about $1.25 trillion. The shortfall lands as public-market investors grow louder about an AI "bubble," with capital rotating toward companies that can show near-term revenue rather than pure compute build-out.

The $1.25 trillion figure itself has been trimmed repeatedly. Reporting citing the Financial Times puts the latest sought valuation about $150 billion below a prior target, with OpenAI reportedly now seeking around $20 billion at that level. Investors said to be involved include Coatue and capital from the UAE.

The practical read: OpenAI is still raising extraordinary sums, but the terms have shifted in investors' favor. A smaller round at a flat-to-lower valuation changes the calculus for every startup that benchmarked its own fundraising against OpenAI's last price.

### 2. Anthropic Files Its S-1, Disclosing $16.3 Billion in First-Half Revenue
Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-frontier-ai-lab-discloses-revenue-and-risks-in-highly-anticipated-s-1-filing-141702607.html) · [Mint](https://www.msn.com/en-in/news/other/anthropic-ipo-it-s-official-now-artificial-intelligence-giant-files-papers-to-sell-shares-to-public-for-first-time/ar-AA2g6lPh) · [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/anthropics-deal-with-elon-musks-spacexai-is-even-bigger-than-we-knew-ipo-filing-shows-2000830618)

Anthropic has officially filed papers to sell shares to the public, becoming the first of the frontier labs to formally enter the IPO pipeline. The S-1 discloses $16.3 billion in revenue for the first half of the year alongside a lengthy risk-factor section covering compute dependence, model-safety liabilities, and customer concentration.

The filing also surfaced a strategic detail that had been underappreciated: Anthropic's compute arrangement with Elon Musk's SpaceXAI is larger than previously known — a reminder that access to training capacity, not just model quality, is the binding constraint in the frontier-lab race.

For the industry, an Anthropic S-1 does two things at once. It gives the public market the first real audited look inside a frontier lab's economics, and it forces OpenAI and others to be measured against a disclosed benchmark rather than rumored revenue figures.

### 3. Google Opens Gemini and Search to Third-Party Apps With a New Connected Apps Platform
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/28/as-ai-agents-reshape-the-web-google-opens-gemini-and-search-to-more-apps/) · [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/connected-apps-one-place/) · [Digital Trends via MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/technology/tech-companies/google-is-making-gemini-and-search-work-better-with-the-apps-you-love/ar-AA2gu7H8)

Google launched a Connected Apps Platform in public preview, letting any U.S. developer submit apps that plug into both the Gemini app and Google Search. Developers integrate through a cloud-hosted MCP server or Android's AppFunctions APIs, then register, test, and submit via the Google Connected Console — and can choose whether their app appears in Search, Gemini, or both.

Once live, developers get dashboards for traffic, latency, and error rates, plus control over verified branding and authenticated accounts. Google says over 50 connected apps are already available in Gemini, from partners including Adobe, Experian, Peloton, and Localiza; those integrations now move into Search over the coming weeks.

The strategic stakes are high. TechCrunch frames connected apps as an early "SEO for the agentic web" — a bid to make Google's AI surfaces, rather than individual apps, the place consumers transact. OpenAI has pursued the same idea with ChatGPT connected apps, and Meta with Muse.

### 4. DOJ Presses Nvidia on Its $12.9 Billion Hugging Face Acquisition
Sources: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-doj-is-inquiring-about-nvidias-purchase-hugging-face-sources-say-2026-10-28/) · [CNBC](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/markets/nvidias-129-billion-hugging-face-deal-draws-doj-scrutiny/ar-AA2ghg6v) · [Barron's](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/nvidia-stock-slips-as-competition-probe-presents-new-ai-concern/ar-AA2gjTJw)

The U.S. Department of Justice has begun inquiring about Nvidia's $12.9 billion purchase of Hugging Face, according to people familiar with the matter. The questions focus on whether the deal would hand Nvidia unfair control over the open-model ecosystem — Hugging Face is the dominant hub where open-weight models, datasets, and evaluation tooling are published.

Nvidia's stock slid on the news, compounding a rough stretch that also included a report of suspected Chinese espionage targeting CEO Jensen Huang. Investors are weighing whether antitrust attention on the Hugging Face deal signals a broader willingness in Washington to test Nvidia's position as the default AI compute vendor.

A Hugging Face acquisition would put Nvidia in control of distribution for the very open models that compete with its customers' closed ones. That vertical integration is precisely what antitrust reviewers tend to examine, and it may extend deal timelines well into next year.

### 5. OpenAI Brings ChatGPT Dot Agents to iMessage and Android RCS
Sources: [Digital Trends via Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/openai-now-lets-text-chatgpt-010639985.html) · [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-dots-imessage-rcs-pro-users/) · [The News International](https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/1421702-you-can-now-text-your-chatgpt-dot-3-things-to-know)

OpenAI is bringing its ChatGPT Dot agents to Apple's iMessage and Android's RCS, letting Pro subscribers text an agent directly to get tasks done instead of opening a dedicated app. The rollout pairs persistent per-user Dot personas with API credits for Pro users, according to coverage of the announcement.

The move is a distribution play. By living inside the default messaging client on both major mobile platforms, OpenAI meets users where they already are — and sidesteps the friction of app switching that has limited consumer AI usage beyond early adopters.

It also puts OpenAI in direct competition with the platforms themselves. Apple and Google both control the messaging surfaces OpenAI is now building on, and both are shipping their own assistant integrations — a dependency worth watching.

### 6. S&P Cuts Meta's Credit Outlook to Negative Over Escalating AI Capex
Sources: [Reuters via MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/top-stocks/s-p-global-revises-meta-s-outlook-to-negative-on-ai-spending-cash-flow-concerns/ar-AA2gre6O) · [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-sp-outlook-negative-ai-spending) · [BW Businessworld](https://www.businessworld.in/article/s-p-revises-meta-outlook-as-ai-spending-pressures-cash-flow-631471)

S&P Global Ratings revised Meta's outlook to negative from stable, citing the strain that AI infrastructure spending is placing on free cash flow. Meta's AA- rating — the fourth-highest investment-grade score — was affirmed, but the negative outlook signals S&P sees meaningful risk of a downgrade if cash generation doesn't keep pace with capex.

The revision follows Meta's plan to raise up to $95 billion to build AI infrastructure, and comes as Nvidia agreed to invest $3 billion in Meta's equity offering as part of an expanded AI partnership. Meta is simultaneously facing scrutiny over scraping websites for AI training data, with an update delayed amid the concerns.

Meta's situation is the clearest illustration yet of how the AI build-out has become a credit-market story, not just an equity one. When a AA- issuer gets a negative outlook over AI spending, the cost of capital for the entire hyperscaler cohort moves with it.

### 7. House Democrats Press OpenAI on Whistleblower Protections Amid Cybersecurity Incidents
Sources: [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-whistleblowers-house-democrats-casar-letter) · [MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/money/general/ai-safety-groups-aren-t-buying-openai-s-spending-retreat/ar-AA2dDlEC)

Twenty House Democrats sent a letter to Sam Altman asking OpenAI to protect whistleblowers and maintain its model pause, following what the letter describes as "a slew of cybersecurity incidents." The push reflects growing congressional attention to how frontier labs handle internal safety dissent and security disclosures.

The letter arrives in a charged context. Safety groups have publicly rejected OpenAI's characterization of a recent spending retreat, arguing that reduced safety investment is being framed as fiscal discipline. British Columbia has separately sued OpenAI over the Tumbler Ridge school shooting, adding legal pressure on the consumer-safety side.

For AI policy watchers, the significance is procedural: Congress is beginning to test how much it can shape lab behavior through disclosure and whistleblower pressure, without passing new statute. That approach faces fewer legislative hurdles than comprehensive AI regulation and may become the default oversight tool.
