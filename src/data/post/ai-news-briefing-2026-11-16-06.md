### 1. Jensen Huang steps down as Nvidia CEO amid scandal
Sources: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/tsmc-invest-up-1-billion-anthropic-openai-2026-11-10/) · [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/video/business/economy/100000011279681/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-resigns-affairs.html) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-affair-with-alleged-chinese-spy-a-timeline/)

Nvidia's board announced that Jensen Huang has resigned as chief executive, capping a stunning reversal for the leader who built the company into the central supplier of the AI boom. The departure follows revelations of a personal relationship with a woman described in reports as an alleged Chinese intelligence asset, and board scrutiny of possible foreign targeting of the CEO. Nvidia shares fell sharply on the news.

The resignation triggered shockwaves across Silicon Valley and Asian markets. Elon Musk and other tech executives signed a petition calling on Nvidia to reinstate Huang, while VCs and founders warned his exit punches a hole in AI startup funding and confidence. Analysts are now focused on succession — Business Insider reports the board is weighing a shortlist of internal executives.

### 2. TSMC approves up to $1B investment in Anthropic and OpenAI
Sources: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/tsmc-invest-up-1-billion-anthropic-openai-2026-11-10/) · [The Wall Street Journal (via Yahoo Finance)](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/tsmc-approves-1-billion-investment-052900007.html) · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/taiwans-chipmaking-giant-tsmc-to-invest-1b-in-anthropic-openai/)

TSMC's board approved a plan to invest up to $1 billion each in Anthropic and OpenAI, deepening the chipmaker's entanglement with the two leading frontier labs it already supplies. The move is widely read as TSMC locking in demand visibility for advanced nodes while securing strategic stakes in the customers driving its AI accelerator volumes.

The investment comes at a delicate moment: Nvidia's leadership turmoil has unsettled the accelerator supply chain, and TSMC is positioning itself as a stabilizing anchor. Financial terms beyond the headline ceiling — including structure and timing — were not fully disclosed.

### 3. Google rolls out Gemini 4 Hydrogen
Sources: [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/11/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-hydrogen-finally-replacing-3-8-flash/) · [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/gemini-4-hydrogen-vs-argon/) · [The Economic Times](https://www.msn.com/en-in/news/other/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-hydrogen-with-25x-speed-boost-four-reasoning-levels/ar-AA2hl40e?ocid=BingNewsVerp)

Google began rolling out Gemini 4 Hydrogen, finally replacing the long-serving 3.8 Flash model. The release claims roughly a 2.5x performance improvement alongside four selectable reasoning levels, and it is being pushed to free-tier users as well as paid subscribers — an unusually aggressive distribution play.

Early third-party evaluations suggest the cheaper Hydrogen model outscores Google's own Argon on some AI rankings, raising questions about internal model positioning. The rollout is being framed as Google's answer to GPT-6.1 and Claude in the escalating frontier race.

### 4. OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet so agents can shop and pay
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/) · [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/cool-tech/chatgpt-wallet-is-here-and-it-wants-your-ai-agent-to-go-shopping-on-your-behalf/) · [CoinCentral](https://coincentral.com/openai-adds-a-card-wallet-to-chatgpt-so-ai-agents-can-pay-for-purchases/)

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, a card-and-checkout layer that lets AI agents complete purchases on a user's behalf. The feature pairs with a purchasing agent (reported as "Dot" in some coverage) that can browse, select, and pay — moving ChatGPT from an assistant that recommends products to one that transacts.

The launch pushes OpenAI directly into commerce and payments infrastructure, an area already contested by Amazon, Google, and a wave of agentic-checkout startups. It also raises fresh questions about liability, fraud, and how much purchasing autonomy users will actually grant a model.

### 5. LangChain raises $300M at a $3B valuation
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/ai-agent-startup-langchain-hits-3b-valuation/) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/langchain-secures-300m-series-c-at-3b-valuation-for-agent-governance/) · [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/langchain-300-million-series-c-funding-led-by-capitalg-at-3-billion-valuation-to-expand-ai-agent-platform/)

LangChain closed a $300 million Series C led by existing investor CapitalG at a $3 billion valuation, with its LangSmith observability platform now used by more than 7,000 organizations. The round signals that investor appetite is shifting from raw model builders toward the tooling that governs, monitors, and controls autonomous agents.

The company says the capital will expand LangSmith and agent-governance capabilities. The raise lands as the agent ecosystem matures from demos into production deployments where auditability and control matter — and as Nvidia's turmoil makes some founders nervous about the broader funding climate.

### 6. FAA expands AI air-traffic advice across entire US airspace
Sources: [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/features/2026/11/faa-expands-ai-advice-to-manage-air-traffic-congestion-across-entire-us-airspace/) · [The Atlanta Journal-Constitution](https://www.ajc.com/business/2026/11/new-ai-tech-air-traffic-control-atlanta/) · [KXAN (via MSN)](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/faa-groundings-follow-congressional-warnings-about-new-ai-air-traffic-system/ar-AA2hoH1g?ocid=BingNewsVerp)

The FAA expanded an AI advisory tool for managing air-traffic congestion from select sites to the entire US airspace, following deployments such as the system now running in Atlanta. The tool is positioned as decision support for controllers rather than autonomous control, but its rapid national scale-up has drawn congressional scrutiny.

Lawmakers had warned about the new AI air-traffic system before a recent nationwide ground stop, and the vendor has publicly denied its tool caused the disruption. The episode has become a test case for how fast AI can be pushed into safety-critical infrastructure.
