## Top 7 Stories

### 1. Anthropic Overtakes OpenAI as World's Most Valuable AI Startup at $965B

Anthropic has officially surpassed OpenAI to become the world's most valuable artificial intelligence startup following its $65 billion Series H funding round, bringing its valuation to an estimated $965 billion. The round, which reportedly included significant investment from Google for compute access, positions Anthropic ahead of OpenAI in market valuation for the first time.

The funding comes as Anthropic's revenue trajectory has accelerated dramatically, with the company now rivaling OpenAI in AI enterprise adoption. An IPO is reportedly being planned, though no timeline has been confirmed. The milestone underscores the intensifying competition between frontier AI labs as the race for AI supremacy enters a new phase.

### 2. Anthropic Releases Claude Opus 4.8 with "Dynamic Workflow" Tool

Anthropic has launched Claude Opus 4.8, its latest flagship model, featuring a groundbreaking "dynamic workflow" capability that allows the AI to autonomously chain together complex multi-step tasks. The new tool enables Claude to plan, execute, and self-correct through intricate workflows without requiring constant human prompting.

The release signals Anthropic's continued push toward agentic AI systems that can operate semi-independently in enterprise environments. Opus 4.8 represents a significant step forward in reliability and task-completion rates, directly competing with OpenAI's latest agent frameworks.

### 3. Google Revamps Search for First Time in 25 Years with AI-Powered Overhaul

Google has fundamentally redesigned its iconic search interface, marking the first major change to the search box in a quarter century. The AI-powered redesign integrates personal AI agents directly into search results, allowing users to interact with contextual AI assistants that can perform actions rather than just return links.

Sundar Pichai emphasized that Google's approach to AI coding agents and search integration aims to keep pace with competitors OpenAI and Anthropic. The shift represents Google's most significant bet on AI-native search, attempting to reclaim its dominance in an era where AI chatbots are increasingly replacing traditional search.

### 4. NVIDIA Doubles Down on AI Infrastructure at Computex 2026

NVIDIA is investing billions into emerging AI infrastructure technologies as Computex 2026 puts the spotlight on Taiwan's expanding role in the global AI supply chain. The company unveiled next-generation AI compute architectures designed to support the exponential growth in training and inference demands from frontier AI labs.

NVIDIA's GTC 2026 strategy focused on redefining the entire AI ecosystem — from compute competition to infrastructure transformation. With the first Windows PC powered by NVIDIA's new AI chips set to debut next week, the company is expanding beyond data centers into consumer and edge AI markets.

### 5. Meta's AI Pendant Leaks as Reality Labs Losses Surpass $4 Billion

Meta is preparing to launch an "AI Pendant" wearable device, according to new reports, even as its Reality Labs division continues to hemorrhage money with losses exceeding $4 billion. The AI Pendant is expected to serve as a portable AI companion, competing with emerging wearable AI devices from other tech giants.

The device reflects Meta's strategic pivot toward hardware-based AI interfaces beyond the metaverse. However, investors continue to scrutinize the mounting losses in Reality Labs, questioning whether Meta's AI hardware bets will deliver returns comparable to its core advertising business.

### 6. AI Agents Could Push College Graduate Unemployment Over 30%, ServiceNow CEO Warns

ServiceNow's CEO has issued a stark warning that agentic AI could drive college graduate unemployment beyond 30%, far exceeding previous estimates of AI's labor market impact. The prediction reflects growing concern that AI agents capable of autonomous task execution will displace not just routine workers but also knowledge professionals.

The warning comes as multiple companies report that using AI agents is becoming cheaper than hiring human employees for certain tasks. Microsoft's internal reports have also highlighted AI's real cost problem, with some organizations finding that human labor remains more economical than AI deployment for complex roles.

### 7. Uber CFO: AI's Impact on Jobs Will Be "Worse Than You Thought"

Uber's CFO has made headlines by stating that AI's impact on employment will be more severe than most analysts currently predict. The candid assessment adds to a growing chorus of corporate leaders warning about the disruptive potential of agentic AI across multiple industries.

The statement reflects a broader shift in corporate messaging — from cautious optimism about AI augmentation to frank acknowledgment of significant workforce disruption. As AI agents become more capable, companies are grappling with how to balance productivity gains against social and economic consequences.
