## Top 7 Stories

### 1. Lean and Mathlib certify the Claude-assisted Riemann hypothesis proof

Sources: [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/lean-mathlib-certify-riemann-hypothesis-proof-alpoge-claude) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1010051/anthropic-riemann-hypothesis-millennium-prize-problem) · [Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-anthropic-claude-math-greatest-riddle.html)

The formal-methods community has moved to validate Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge's claim that he and Claude produced a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, the 1859 conjecture and one of the seven Millennium Prize problems. According to TNW, the Lean and Mathlib groups said they have "full confidence" in the Claude-assisted proof after machine certification, a meaningful step beyond an unverified announcement. The Verge and Phys.org both report that the claim — first floated by Alpöge on Friday — has electrified mathematicians, with supporters framing it as a landmark for AI-assisted discovery and skeptics cautioning that machine-checked proofs still require human review of definitions and axioms.

If it holds, the result would be among the most consequential mathematical demonstrations ever tied to an AI system, and it lands as a live test case for how the field evaluates machine-generated proofs. Expect scrutiny to focus on the Lean formalization's assumptions rather than the natural-language write-up. The episode has also shifted the public conversation from "can AI do math" to "how do we audit AI math," a governance question now moving from philosophy into practice.

### 2. Three days later, Claude is credited with a second major math result (VP vs VNP)

Sources: [Yahoo Tech / BeInCrypto](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/3-days-riemann-hypothesis-claude-100454424.html) · [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-researcher-claims-claude-helped-him-prove-the-riemann-hypothesis)

Just days after the Riemann announcement, a second claim emerged: that Claude helped prove VP versus VNP, the algebraic cousin of P versus NP, according to reporting relayed via Yahoo Tech/BeInCrypto with background from TNW's Riemann coverage. The report explicitly notes that no human has yet checked the second proof — a crucial caveat that distinguishes it from the Lean-certified Riemann work. The rapid succession of claims is already reshaping how researchers think about AI's role in theoretical computer science and cryptography.

The story matters less for any single theorem than for the pattern: frontier labs are now producing mathematical claims at a cadence that traditional peer review cannot absorb. That raises immediate questions about publication norms, priority disputes, and how cryptographic assumptions — many rooted in problems like P vs NP — should respond. Analysts are watching whether Lean-style formal verification becomes the de facto gatekeeper for AI-originated proofs.

### 3. OpenAI rolls GPT-6.2 Sol out as ChatGPT's default model

Sources: [The Mac Observer](https://www.macobserver.com/news/openai-launches-gpt-6-2-sol-in-chatgpt-with-major-vision-and-search-upgrades/) · [Mashable](https://me.mashable.com/tech/78656/openai-upgrades-chatgpts-default-chat-experience-with-faster-smarter-gpt-62-sol-model) · [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/openai-upgrades-chatgpt-to-gpt-62-but-free-users-will-have-to-wait/)

OpenAI has upgraded the default ChatGPT experience to GPT-6.2 Sol, following last week's debut of the model family inside ChatGPT Work and Codex. The Mac Observer reports the rollout brings major upgrades to image understanding and smarter web search, while Mashable frames it as a faster, more capable baseline for everyday users. Neowin adds the important caveat that paid tiers get it first and free users will have to wait.

The move consolidates OpenAI's consumer flagship around a single "Sol" model while keeping the higher-end Astra/Sol variants reserved for Work and Codex customers — a tiering strategy that mirrors competitors' approach to monetizing frontier capability. It also intensifies the head-to-head with Google's Gemini 4 line and Anthropic's Claude, as all three push faster, cheaper default models. Enterprises evaluating assistant vendors now have a fresh baseline to benchmark.

Federal prosecutors now say Abdulrahman Alfozan, the former OpenAI employee accused of planting a hidden recording device in an executive conference room, captured conversations with OpenAI customers as well as discussions covered by attorney-client privilege, according to Gizmodo. Engadget reports Alfozan allegedly uploaded files to Dropbox and has been linked to the Saudi government, while Bleeping Computer documented the original arrest and the device's placement in a sensitive conference room.

Expect labs to tighten conference-room policies and background checks, and watch for whether the case prompts broader disclosure requirements.
