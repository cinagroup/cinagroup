## Top 7 Stories

1. **Claude Cowork Finally Remembers What You Told It in Chat.** TechCrunch reports that Anthropic's Claude Cowork agent now retains memory of what users tell it in conversation, ending a key friction point for the app. Persistent memory is a major upgrade for agentic workflows, letting the assistant carry project context, preferences, and decisions across sessions without re-explaining. The feature brings Claude Cowork closer to the always-on assistant experience Anthropic has been pushing toward in its enterprise push.

2. **Stability AI Raises $76 Million in Fresh Funding.** The maker of Stable Diffusion has closed a $76 million round, per TechCrunch, as the generative image market consolidates around a handful of players. The raise gives Stability runway to keep shipping open-model image generation amid intense competition from larger labs and startups. It also signals that specialized model companies can still attract capital in a funding environment increasingly focused on agents and enterprise AI.

3. **Robotics Startup Generalist Hits $3 Billion Valuation.** TechCrunch reports, citing sources, that Generalist — a robotics startup building general-purpose AI for physical machines — has reached a $3 billion valuation. The round underscores surging investor appetite for embodied AI and robots that can generalize across tasks rather than being hard-coded for single use cases. Robotics has become one of the hottest corners of the AI market as foundation-model techniques move into the physical world.

4. **OpenAI Head of Product Says 'The World Seems to Be Ready' for Agents.** In a TechCrunch interview, OpenAI's head of product Thibault Sottiaux made the case that the market is now ready for agentic AI at scale. The comments come as OpenAI pushes agents across ChatGPT, Work, and its enterprise lineup, betting that autonomous multi-step tasks will be the next major adoption wave. It is the latest signal that agent readiness — not just model capability — is the industry's defining commercial question.

5. **Accel-Backed Keenable Is Indexing the Web for AI Agents.** TechCrunch reports that Keenable, backed by Accel, is building an index of the web designed specifically for AI agents to query. The startup is targeting a gap between human-oriented search and the structured, machine-readable data that autonomous agents need to complete tasks reliably. If agent traffic becomes a meaningful share of web usage, infrastructure like Keenable's could become a critical layer of the agent economy.

6. **Situational Awareness, Star AI Hedge Fund, Is Now Being Probed by the SEC.** TechCrunch reports that Situational Awareness — the AI hedge fund that nearly imploded — is now under investigation by the SEC. The fund, which became famous for the scale of its AI-driven trading ambitions, now faces regulatory scrutiny on top of its operational turmoil. The probe adds to a growing pattern of regulators examining how AI firms handle risk, disclosure, and market impact.

7. **Instinct's Powerful AI Assistant Is Raising Privacy and Security Concerns.** TechCrunch reports that Instinct's AI assistant — notable for how deeply it can act on a user's behalf — is drawing privacy and security scrutiny. The concerns center on the assistant's access to sensitive personal data and the potential for abuse if its capabilities are misused. The story highlights the widening tension between agent autonomy and user safety as assistants grow more powerful.
