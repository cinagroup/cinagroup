## Top 7 Stories

### 1. OpenAI pauses $200 ChatGPT Pro sign-ups as 'unprecedented' Astra demand strains capacity

OpenAI has paused new sign-ups for its $200-per-month ChatGPT Pro tier after what it described as unprecedented demand for its newly released Astra model. According to Fortune, the company has expanded available compute 9.5-fold since 2023 but still cannot keep pace with the load, forcing it to throttle new Pro 20X account creation while it adds more capacity.

The pause is a notable admission that even the best-funded AI lab in the industry remains compute-constrained at the frontier. It also signals that consumer and enterprise appetite for the newest reasoning models continues to outrun infrastructure build-out, a dynamic that has driven much of the sector's capital expenditure over the past two years.

For competitors, the constraint is an opening: Google, Anthropic, and Meta can pitch available capacity as a differentiator while OpenAI rations access to its top-tier product.

### 2. Anthropic blocks accounts over potentially dangerous biological research

Anthropic said it blocked a set of Claude accounts after users sought assistance with potentially dangerous biological research. In its threat intelligence report, the company disclosed that it had disrupted five possible attempts to research bioweapons using Claude, alongside instances of targeted influence operations, weapons-related requests, and mass fraud.

The disclosure is one of the more detailed public accounts of real-world misuse attempts against a frontier model. Anthropic framed the findings as evidence that its safety classifiers and monitoring systems are catching harmful use before it escalates.

The report lands amid a broader industry debate over how much autonomy AI systems should be granted and what monitoring obligations should apply to model providers.

Source: [PC Mag via Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/anthropic-blocked-5-possible-attempts-113612513.html) · [Becker's Hospital Review](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/anthropic-blocks-claude-accounts-over-potentially-dangerous-biological-research/)

### 3. Anthropic accuses DeepSeek and Alibaba of using Claude to train rival models

Anthropic's threat intelligence report also alleged that Chinese AI developers DeepSeek and Alibaba used Claude to help train their own competing models at scale. According to reporting by International Business Times, Anthropic linked Alibaba to more than 151 million Claude exchanges and DeepSeek to another 12.1 million.

The allegation puts a concrete number on the long-simmering concern that frontier labs' outputs are being harvested to bootstrap rival systems — activity that typically violates provider terms of service. It also sharpens the competitive framing between US and Chinese labs, coming as analysts note DeepSeek and Alibaba closing the capability gap.

Expect the claim to intensify scrutiny of API abuse detection and of the enforcement mechanisms labs can realistically apply once training has already occurred.

Source: [International Business Times](https://www.ibtimes.com/deepseek-alibaba-are-closing-ai-gap-anthropic-accuses-them-using-claude-help-train-their-3807381)

### 4. 'Dario is right': Musk and Altman back Anthropic CEO on slowing AI down

Elon Musk and Sam Altman publicly backed Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's call to slow the pace of AI capability improvement, after Amodei warned the race must decelerate amid growing safety fears. Amodei's argument: "We must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models."

The endorsements are striking given the competitive posture of the companies involved — OpenAI and xAI are both racing to ship frontier models, and Altman's agreement with Amodei marks a rare moment of public alignment between rivals on safety framing.

Whether the rhetoric translates into practice remains the open question, particularly as OpenAI rations access to its newest model rather than slowing its rollout.

Source: [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/article/dario-is-right-musk-and-altman-back-anthropic-ceo-on-slowing-ai-down-182008851.html) · [The Independent](https://www.msn.com/en-ca/technology/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-chief-warns-artificial-intelligence-race-must-slow/ar-AA2c5gzs)

### 5. OpenAI pushes for mandatory national AI safety rules

OpenAI said it is pushing for mandatory national AI safety requirements, according to Reuters. The move places the company in the unusual position of advocating for binding regulation rather than voluntary standards.

A single national framework would preempt a growing patchwork of state-level AI laws that labs argue creates compliance complexity — and it comes as federal preemption efforts have drawn pushback from state lawmakers.

The lobbying push also positions OpenAI to help shape the substance of the rules it would ultimately have to follow, a dynamic critics of industry influence have flagged repeatedly.

Source: [Reuters on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/openai-pushes-for-mandatory-national-ai-safety-rules/ar-AA2bVlhR?ocid=BingNewsVerp)

### 6. Meta launches consumer AI agent that can send emails and make payments

Meta rolled out a long-touted AI assistant that can autonomously access other applications to send emails and make payments, Reuters reported. The launch makes Meta one of the first major consumer platforms to ship an agent with direct transactional reach into third-party services.

The agent also generated immediate controversy: Meta said it is changing its AI suggestions after a viral video showed Meta AI prompting a user to identify a child in one of her videos, raising privacy concerns.

The dual rollout — genuine capability plus a fast privacy backlash — illustrates the tension facing consumer agent products, where broad permissions are necessary for usefulness but create obvious misuse and privacy surface area.

Source: [Reuters on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-ca/technology/artificial-intelligence/meta-launches-ai-agent-that-can-access-other-apps-to-send-emails-make-payments/ar-AA2bPkqb?ocid=BingNewsVerp) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/993974/meta-ai-prompt-invasive-suggestions)

### 7. d-Matrix to adopt Nvidia chip-linking tech for AI inference servers

Chip startup d-Matrix said it will adopt Nvidia's NVLink Fusion chip-linking technology, integrating its Raptor XPUs with Nvidia infrastructure for faster deployment of inference systems. The partnership, reported by Reuters, extends Nvidia's interconnect ecosystem to a third-party inference chip vendor.

For Nvidia, opening NVLink to partners reinforces its position as the connective tissue of AI datacenters even as competitors target the inference market where custom silicon is gaining ground. For d-Matrix, the deal offers an easier path into deployments already standardized on Nvidia hardware.

The arrangement lands amid ongoing debate over Nvidia's pricing power, with short-seller Jim Chanos questioning the company's "rentable compute" thesis after Jensen Huang described Nvidia chips as highly rentable.

Source: [Reuters on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-gb/news/other/chip-startup-d-matrix-to-use-nvidia-chip-linking-tech-in-ai-servers/ar-AA2bXaoy?ocid=BingNewsVerp) · [The Economic Times on MSN](https://www.msn.com/en-in/news/other/ai-chipmaker-d-matrix-to-connect-next-gen-inference-chips-to-nvidia-infrastructure/ar-AA2c0vjL?ocid=BingNewsVerp)
