## Top 7 Stories

### 1. Jensen Huang Is Out at Nvidia as Espionage Scandal Widens

Huang's resignation as Nvidia's founding CEO is now the dominant story in AI, with reporting centered on an alleged affair with a woman described in multiple accounts as a Chinese intelligence asset, and on claims that billions of dollars worth of export-controlled AI chips were funneled to China. Wired described the episode as a "$470 billion affair" in market-cap terms, while CNN framed the central unanswered question as "who lied to whom" — a $1 trillion question for Nvidia and Huang alike. A U.S. Senate probe that raked Nvidia's security posture over the coals delivered a second blow the same week.

### 2. Silicon Valley Rallies Behind Huang — and Petitions Nvidia to Reinstate Him

The reaction from the AI industry was immediate and unusually public. Elon Musk and a roster of other tech executives signed a petition calling on Nvidia's board to reinstate Huang, and Wired reported that Silicon Valley figures continued to defend him through the week. Asian governments issued their own statements on the exit, signalling the degree to which Huang had become a diplomatic as well as a corporate figure. The episode has opened a broader debate about founder-run chip companies and whether one person can carry an entire supply chain's strategic risk.

### 3. OpenAI Raises $15B at a $1.25T Valuation — Half of What It Sought

OpenAI closed a $15 billion funding round at a $1.25 trillion private valuation, according to multiple reports, with the company framing the raise around eventual IPO plans. The number is impressive in isolation but telling in context: it is reportedly about half the amount OpenAI had been seeking, and it landed alongside reporting that OpenAI's revenue has reached roughly $10 billion while losses have run to about $10.9 billion. The gap between the raise and the ask is the clearest signal yet that private-market appetite for frontier-lab burn rates has a ceiling.

### 4. Advocacy Groups Sue Five AI Developers to Freeze Frontier Development

Public Citizen and a coalition of advocacy organizations filed suit against OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, and SpaceX's AI division, seeking to halt development of new frontier models. The complaint argues that the major developers cannot control their own technology, and that the risks of continued development without demonstrated containment justify judicial intervention. Protests accompanied the filing. It is an untested legal theory — courts have been reluctant to police internal R&D — but it is the most aggressive attempt yet to use litigation, rather than regulation, as the brake on frontier AI.

Sources: [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/383270/openai-anthropic-google-ai-giants-lawsuit-development-freeze) · [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/nonprofit-groups-sue-openai-google-anthropic-meta-and-spacexs-ai-division-seeking-halt-on-ai-development-101794521310533.html) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/public-citizen-advocacy-groups-sue-to-freeze-frontier-ai-development/)

### 5. Anthropic Expands Claude Into Live Meetings

Anthropic launched Claude Meetings, which lets Claude join live Google Meet calls to take notes and summarise in the room, alongside a wider expansion of Claude's workplace features aimed squarely at Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. The move pushes Anthropic from a chat assistant into ambient meeting infrastructure — a competitive lane where Google and Microsoft already have significant home-field advantage. Analysts read the product drop as a statement of intent about Anthropic's enterprise positioning ahead of its IPO.

Sources: [Mashable](https://me.mashable.com/tech/78275/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-ai-joins-live-google-meet-calls-to-take-notes) · [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/anthropic-expands-claudes-workplace-arsenal-133007151.html)

### 6. ChatGPT's New Wallet Lets AI Agents Shop and Check Out for You

OpenAI debuted a ChatGPT payment method built with the major card networks, letting AI agents complete purchases and check out on a user's behalf. The launch reframes ChatGPT as a transactional layer — an agentic commerce platform rather than a conversation surface — and puts OpenAI into direct contact with the payments stack and its fraud and liability regimes. It also raises a new class of consumer-protection questions about authorising non-human actors to spend money.

Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/openai-debuts-new-chatgpt-payment-method-with-card-networks)

### 7. Google Ships Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Pushes Gemini Into Cars and Glasses

Google rolled out Gemini 4 Hydrogen, finally replacing the long-serving 3.8 Flash model, and simultaneously pushed Gemini into new form factors — Apple CarPlay with hands-free in-car features, and Gemini-powered smartglasses debuting at $395 with a privacy-forward pitch. The CarPlay launch is notable because it puts Google's assistant inside Apple's ecosystem on terms Apple controls. Google is clearly optimising for distribution breadth over model-launch spectacle this cycle.
