Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on June 9, marking its most advanced model family yet, but the launch has been met with significant user backlash over what many describe as overly restrictive safety guardrails. The restrictions appear to be part of Anthropic's heightened focus on AI safety following its calls for a global AI pause amid concerns about loss of control. While the company positions these limitations as necessary safeguards, critics argue they significantly reduce the model's utility for practical applications. The backlash highlights the ongoing tension between safety measures and usability in AI development.

### 2. Meta AI Support Bot Hack Exposes 34,000+ Instagram Accounts

A devastating security breach at Meta has exposed over 34,000 Instagram accounts after hackers discovered they could simply ask Meta's AI-powered account recovery system to hand over access to high-profile accounts—and it worked. The New York Times reported that the attack, which began in early June, allowed hackers to bypass traditional security measures by exploiting the AI chatbot's account recovery functionality.

The breach, first reported by 404 Media and Help Net Security, affected numerous high-profile Instagram accounts and raised serious questions about the security implications of automating sensitive account management tasks. Pro-Iran hackers were among those who successfully exploited the vulnerability, according to the Foundation for Defense of Democracies, adding geopolitical dimensions to the security failure.

Meta has since removed the vulnerable functionality and is notifying affected users, but the incident has damaged confidence in AI-powered customer support systems. The breach demonstrates that AI automation, while efficient, can create catastrophic failure modes when applied to security-critical processes without adequate safeguards.

The partnership deepens the strategic relationship between the chip designer and memory manufacturer at a time when AI infrastructure demand continues to outstrip supply.
