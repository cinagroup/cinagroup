1. **Gemini Minecraft experiment — core results supported; cost scope wrong.** [Google researchers' preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22319), submitted October 15, describes four agents, a randomized Easy world, Mineflayer, 27 hours 14 minutes to victory, 2,783 programs, 229 messages, and 605 deaths. The **four-player run through victory** is estimated at $520 at introductory pricing ($1,040 standard). The $810 introductory estimate includes the Observer and a further day of activity, not merely the victory run; standard full-session price is $1,620. An Observer cannot prove absence of cheating independently of the researchers' own setup, and one run is not a general reliability benchmark.
