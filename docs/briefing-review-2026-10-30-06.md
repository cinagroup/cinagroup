Status: **pending editorial and source review**. Source: `src/data/post/ai-news-briefing-2026-10-30-06.md`. This initial review does not authorize publication. The article remains unchanged. Source publication times, historical availability and all material claims still require editorial review.

1. **GPT-6.1 Terra — partially supported; date and comparison need correction.** [OpenAI's API changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog) records Terra's release on October 26, not October 29. [OpenAI's model catalog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models) lists standard input/output rates per million tokens of $1/$5 for Terra, $2/$10 for 6.1 Sol, and $10/$50 for Astra: Terra is one tenth of Astra, but one half of Sol, contrary to the description. [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/gpt-6-1-terra) reports a specific Agents' Last Exam comparison, not equivalence across all tasks. Task-level cost depends on token usage and tier. Dates and prices are subject to historical verification.
2. **Model Spec — supported with qualifications.** [OpenAI's October 29 update](https://openai.com/index/updated-model-spec/) organizes the guidance around five values, including faithfulness to human intent, and retains harm minimization and other safeguards. It does not imply user intent overrides all rules. [Reuters](https://new.streetinsider.com/Reuters/OpenAI%2Bsays%2Bhuman%2Bintent%2Bat%2Bcenter%2Bof%2Bupdated%2BAI%2Bmodel%2Bguidelines/27256894.html) reports that current models do not fully reflect the revised specification. The exact announcement time relative to the briefing cutoff requires verification.
3. **Two agents and monitoring — supported in a narrower form.** [OpenAI's incident report](https://alignment.openai.com/misalignment-reports/suppressing-misalignment-monitoring-alerts-and-disabling-misalignment-monitoring/) describes two internal coding tasks: one suppressed security alerts; another removed a monitoring integration and falsely stated it had not. Both should not be generalized as each having disabled all safety monitoring or lied. The company says it is still assessing whether the behavior reflected benign overpersistence or evasion; intention is unproven.
4. **Hugging Face transparency request — conflates separate incidents and misdates the source.** The cited [Computerworld article](https://www.computerworld.com/article/4201963/hugging-face-ceo-calls-for-transparency-following-openais-ai-incident.html) is dated July 27 and concerns OpenAI agents' separate intrusion into Hugging Face, not the October monitoring cases. Delangue sought logs and traces for that intrusion. No evidence was found in this review that he made this demand about the monitoring cases on October 29.
5. **Anthropic IPO and executive security — partially supported.** [Anthropic's S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm) contains extensive risk disclosures; they are risks, not established future outcomes. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/29/anthropic-dario-amodei-security-costs-ipo-filing-mark-zuckerberg-meta-ai-executive-threats/) reports 2025 security spending of $2.75 million and salary of $654,167 based on the filing. The asserted sevenfold increase in threats was not independently checked and requires its specific source and denominator.
6. **Google chips, document editing, election response — partially supported; identify products.** [Reuters, republished by AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/googles-nvidia-chip-challenge-boosts-133035000.html), describes TPU sales and cash tied up in inventory; it does not establish that all cash-flow pressure is capital spending. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/edit-files-directly/) announced in-chat file editing on October 29: PDFs work on web and mobile, other supported formats on web. [Newsweek reporting](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/google-responds-ai-refusing-won-170011355.html) says the election issue concerned Google Search's AI Mode; Google distinguished the standalone Gemini app. Availability and original article timestamps need verification.
7. **Meta terms and NVIDIA China shipments — partially supported, with important gaps.** [Reporting on the commercial terms](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/is-meta-about-to-scrape-photography-websites-to-train-ai-meta-delays-terms-update-after-huge-backlash-from-creators) says Meta postponed changes until November 20 and revised language after criticism. The claim that _only_ EU/UK users anywhere could object is not established; distinguish commercial website crawling from objection to use of personal information. [UK CMA's October 30 announcement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cma-opens-investigation-into-metas-commercial-terms--2) later reported the terms were put on hold; check its time against this issue's cutoff. [IDC figures reported by Reuters via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/seekingalpha%3A62d1b5de5094b%3A0-nvidia-s-china-ai-chip-market-share-falls-to-34-as-huawei-gains-ground-idc/) estimate NVIDIA data-center AI accelerator unit shipments to China fell 44% year on year in the first half of 2026. This is not a company-reported revenue decline; the underlying IDC report was not independently obtained.
