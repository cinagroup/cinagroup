All page headers use the official horizontal lockup containing the original
Chinese `海内集团` lettering and the custom rounded Geist Regular `CINAGROUP`
wordmark. The v2.1.1 English artwork preserves the two sharp inner joins in the
letter N. Lettering is part of the supplied PNG and does not depend on web fonts.
The 832 × 288 PNG is displayed at 56 CSS pixels tall with its original aspect ratio
and safety padding preserved.
Dark mode uses the matching official white-lettering lockup; the color symbol is
unchanged between themes. No separate typeset company name is added.

The horizontal lockups and favicons retain the official corner radius of
4.6875% of the blue symbol's side length. Corners scale with the artwork; no
additional CSS rounding or clipping is applied to the combined logo.

Favicons, the touch icon, and the square logo used by structured data use the
matching v2 artwork. The historical white transparent mark is retained as an
unused compatibility asset.
